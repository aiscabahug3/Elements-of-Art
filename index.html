The 7 Elements of Art

Line

A line can show movement and emotion!

Shape

Shapes are everywhere—in nature and in our world!

Form

A form is a 3D shape.

Color

Colors can set a mood (e.g. happy or sad).

Texture

Texture can make art look rough, smooth, or fuzzy.

Value

Value helps show depth and form.

Space

Space helps create balance in art.

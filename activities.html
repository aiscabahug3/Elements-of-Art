Art Detective: Find the Element!

Read each clue and identify the element of art

Activities

Welcome to Art Detective!

In this online activity, you will look at short descriptions and identify the element of art being described. You can complete the activity directly on the website using your phone, tablet, or computer. No physical materials or recording are needed.

Learning Objectives

After completing this activity, learners are expected to:

  • Identify the elements of art from simple descriptions.
  • Choose the correct element based on its characteristics.
  • Explain how elements of art appear in everyday objects.

How to Play

  1. Read each clue on the screen.
  2. Choose the element of art that matches the clue.
  3. Click the "Check Answer" button to see if your answer is correct.
  4. Continue until you finish all five questions and view your score.
